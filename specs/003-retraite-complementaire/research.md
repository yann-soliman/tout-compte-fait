## Annual estimate and rounding

- Decision: exact annual mathematical model with known source parameters; complementary confidence `estimated`; points displayed to nearest0.01, pension nearest0.01 €, half-up application presentation conventions. Do not apply the display-point rounding before pension valuation.
- Rationale: periodic actual payments and caisse rounding cannot be reconstructed from annual salary/CA alone. CNAV2025-23 page10 shows quarterly global contributions rounded to euros, annual allocated amounts rounded to euros and6.50 rounded to7 RCI points in a2023 example. DILA F23369 instead shows whole-point floor for BIC examples. Neither establishes a unique2026 BNC rounding/payment sequence for this annual input.
- Alternatives rejected: arbitrary normative whole-point floor/nearest, invented quarterly splits, blocking all useful indicative rights despite verified parameters.
- UI limitation: annual estimate, contributions assumed paid, personal prorated ceilings/payroll specifics not reproduced; pension reference values not future guarantees.
- Base quarters and income remain existing independent calculations; no point-based base pension fabricated.
